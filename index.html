What is a SIEM and why does it matter?
A SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) collects logs from all systems and lets analysts search for threats using queries. Splunk uses SPL and Microsoft Sentinel uses KQL — both are used by Nigerian banks, fintechs and global enterprises.
1
Browse the sample logs to understand what data is available
2
Write a query to find threats, or click a snippet to load one
3
Press Run Query — AI evaluates your query against the real log data
Splunk SPL appears in 78% of Nigerian SOC job postings. Sentinel KQL is used by Access Bank, GTBank and Flutterwave.

How Alert Triage Works
In a real SOC, dozens of alerts fire daily. Your job is to decide what each one means:
!
True Positive — Real attack. Take immediate action: block, isolate, escalate.
✓
False Positive — Looks suspicious but has a harmless explanation. Tune your rules.
↑
Escalate — Too complex or serious for L1. Send to senior analyst with evidence.
In Nigerian bank interviews, triage scenarios are extremely common. Practise until you can decide in under 30 seconds.

What is Prompt Injection?
Prompt injection is a cybersecurity attack that manipulates AI systems by inserting malicious instructions. It is one of the fastest-growing attack vectors as AI is used in real systems.
1
Select an AI target — each has a hidden system prompt protecting it
2
Choose a technique to understand how it works, then craft your payload
3
If the AI violates its instructions, you earn XP. Read the analysis to learn why it worked.
AI red teaming is a paid specialisation. Successful injectors earn $100-$10,000 per vulnerability on bug bounty platforms.
